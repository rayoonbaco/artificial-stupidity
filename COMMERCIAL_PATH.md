# Public core, paid implementation

## Public research edition

Keep the prototype, protocol, tests, bounded evidence, and claim boundaries freely inspectable. Public evidence makes the idea legible, testable, and credible.

## Potential paid work

Organizations may pay for work around the public core:

- private deployment inside their environment;
- integration with model, agent, data, and approval workflows;
- customized evidence and escalation rules;
- audit-ready reports and provenance capture;
- domain-specific evaluation design;
- implementation support, training, and maintenance.

## Pilot language

"The public research edition is free to inspect and reproduce. I am also open to a small number of paid organizational pilots involving private deployment, customized evidence rules, audit-ready reporting, integration, and support."

This is an invitation to discuss scoped services, not a posted price, guarantee, certification, or claim of production readiness.
