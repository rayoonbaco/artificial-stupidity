# Protected-holdout evidence note

**ARTIFICIAL STUPIDITY - A MONAHINGA™ Evidence Project**

The protected-holdout result is fully incorporated into the second-edition dossier and executive summary included in this repository. This short note remains only as a navigation aid for readers of earlier packages.

The preregistered follow-up used one NVIDIA H100 SXM and three interleaved baseline/candidate pairs. Median validation BPB improved from 1.000213 to 0.990934 (0.928%), and median protected-holdout BPB improved from 0.999357 to 0.990126 (0.924%). Every candidate beat every baseline on both metrics, all six runs completed successfully, and peak VRAM fell 0.337%.

The original machine decision was ESCALATE because human comprehensibility was unknown. Raymond Anthony Gomez separately reviewed the evidence and one-line change and authorized KEEP on September 13, 2026.

Complete raw evidence and decision records are under `benchmark/evidence/h100_sxm_20260913/`.
