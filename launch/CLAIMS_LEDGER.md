# Public claims ledger

## Safe, directly supported claims

- The repository implements an external KEEP / REJECT / ESCALATE gate.
- The GPU-free suite contains 27 passing tests in the packaged environment.
- The protected-holdout protocol ran three interleaved baseline/candidate pairs on one H100 SXM.
- Median validation BPB improved 0.928%; median holdout BPB improved 0.924%.
- Every candidate beat every baseline on both metrics, producing 9/9 pairwise wins for each.
- Peak VRAM was 0.337% lower for the candidate.
- All six training runs exited successfully.
- A post-run import error occurred, was disclosed, and was repaired without more training.
- The machine escalated because human comprehensibility was unknown; Raymond Anthony Gomez separately authorized KEEP.

## Claims that require qualifiers

- "Protected holdout" means sequestered from training, tokenizer preparation, and ordinary validation paths. It was not cryptographically secret.
- The candidate is better only within the tested model, data, hardware, and five-minute budget.
- The gate is an evidence-discipline prototype, not a demonstrated general AI-safety system.
- The architecture is an original synthesis and implementation claim, not a claim to have coined "artificial stupidity" or invented critique, abstention, or human oversight.

## Claims not to make

- "Proven to make AI safe."
- "The first use of artificial stupidity."
- "Universally improves AI models."
- "Independently peer reviewed."
- "Endorsed by Andrej Karpathy, OpenAI, RunPod, or any cited author."
- "MONAHINGA is a registered trademark" or use of the registration symbol.
