# Prototype pass contract

- **Decision capability:** determine whether a nominal `val_bpb` improvement is
  sufficiently supported to keep, must be rejected, or needs human review.
- **User and use:** an autonomous-research operator selecting the next accepted
  experiment in Karpathy's `autoresearch` loop.
- **Source of truth:** upstream commit
  `228791fb499afffb54b46200aca536f79142f117`, its fixed evaluation output, the
  protected gate configuration, and evidence produced by reproducible checks.
- **Delta:** add a non-editable acceptance boundary without changing upstream
  `prepare.py`, `train.py`, or `program.md`.
- **Files changed:** additive `gate/`, tests, companion instructions, derivative
  README section, and provenance notice.
- **Acceptance tests:** supported gain keeps; regression rejects; critical
  failure rejects; unknown required evidence escalates; missing falsifiability
  escalates; malformed or impossible inputs fail closed; upstream core files
  remain byte-identical.
- **Excluded:** new training architecture, safety certification, or empirical
  proof that the gate improves real-world safety. The predeclared
  protected-holdout experiment now passes its bounded empirical conditions;
  broader generalization remains a separate question. Named human authorization
  was completed on September 13, 2026.
- **Recovery:** remove the additive files or return to the pinned upstream
  commit; the upstream core remains unchanged.
- **Cost class:** local prototype plus one paid same-hardware H100 benchmark.
- **Readiness:** 97/100 for the bounded research probe; below deployment
  readiness until evidence fields are bound to protected executable tests.

## Benchmark contract

- Replay the same ordered candidate records through the upstream metric rule
  and the independent gate.
- Report original keeps, gated keeps, rejects, escalations, and challenged keeps.
- Never relabel incomplete evidence as a proven failure.
- Synthetic records validate the harness only. The included H100 SXM evidence
  supports repeatable validation and protected-holdout improvement for one
  candidate under the bounded protocol in `benchmark/H100_RERUN_PROTOCOL.md`.
- The protected rerun uses three interleaved runs per arm and requires every
  candidate score to beat every baseline score on both validation and holdout.
- The runner packages complete raw logs, environment records, source snapshots,
  hashes, partial failures, and structured decisions automatically.
- The machine cannot mark human comprehensibility as passed; final KEEP remains
  a named human authorization.

## Handoff rule

- Every distributable project update must be packaged as one complete ZIP file.
- Give Ray a direct chat download link to that ZIP so he can save it to his
  Windows **Downloads** folder.
- Do not use a Library link as the primary handoff and do not require Ray to
  reconstruct the project from patches or individual files.
- The ZIP must contain the full current project, preserve the upstream files,
  exclude `.git`, caches, and generated bytecode, and pass an archive integrity
  check before delivery.
